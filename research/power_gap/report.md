## Interpretation

- This split tests whether large Power Gaps have a different 45–60 session chart signature from ordinary 4–7% gaps.
- Features are inherited from the matched-control study and use only information available before the gap day.
- If the >=15% bucket shows a materially stronger signature, the next scanner should target Power Gaps rather than all gaps.
